About me

I am a marine ecologist with a PhD from Umeå University. My work focuses on phytoplankton ecology, marine environmental monitoring and the analysis of oceanographic observations.

I am developing a portfolio of reproducible environmental data analyses using Python, SQL and GIS.

Projects

Baltic Sea Environmental Data Exploration

An educational Python project demonstrating data inspection, filtering, station-level summaries, missing-value assessment, basic quality control and vertical profile visualization.

Data: a synthetic training dataset inspired by Baltic Sea monitoring, not actual B15 or BY5 observations.

Tools: Python, Pandas, NumPy, Matplotlib and Jupyter Notebook.

Notebook and figures will be added as the project develops.
